HOW IT WORKS

  1. Fork the repo. github.com/hackclub/receipt -- everything you need is in editor/. It has to be this starter because my printer is fussy. Sowwy

  2. Make it yours. Edit sketch.js in your normal editor and save; check the corresponding preview. Pick a seed you like, hit Export PNG

  3. Ship it. Send your repo link and that PNG here and wait! I'll print it on a receipt printer and send it to you. Maybe I'll drop some stickers too ;>

QUESTIONS >_<

What can I make?

Anything that can fit on a receipt that you can done w/ p5.js!

384 pixels wide!

What do I submit?

A public link to your repo and the PNG exported by the editor. Select your Hackatime project when submitting. Submit here.

Yep! I'll print your art on a receipt printer and mail it to you.
